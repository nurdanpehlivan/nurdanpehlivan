## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🥗 Frizzy (Shelfie) — Smart Kitchen & Food Waste Tracker
`Kotlin` `Compose` `Hilt` `Room` `Gemini` `ML Kit` `Cloudflare Workers + D1`

Taken from idea to **Google Play** end-to-end.
- Multi-module Clean Architecture (core domain/data/ui + **8 feature modules**), build-logic convention plugins, MVI-style Contract / ViewModel / Screen
- **Gemini API** product recognition from photos & recipe suggestions, **ML Kit OCR** for expiry dates, Open Food Facts barcode lookup
- WorkManager notifications, **Glance widget**, Play Billing subscriptions, Cloudflare Workers + D1 backend for sync & family sharing

### 📔 [Mimo](https://github.com/nurdanpehlivan/Mimo) — Journal & Life Tracker
`Kotlin` `Compose` `Room + SQLCipher` `Firebase AI Logic` `Health Connect` `Media3`

Notes, photos, voice, mood, sleep and spending in one timeline — in **4 languages**.
- Encrypted database with **SQLCipher** (key in Android Keystore), biometric lock & encrypted backups
- Gemini-powered calorie estimation from meal photos and speech-to-text; Health Connect, Maps Compose & Media3 integrations

### ⛅ [Nimbus](https://github.com/nurdanpehlivan/KampWeather-App) — Weather App
`Kotlin` `Compose` `Retrofit` `StateFlow` `DataStore`

Built during the Kamp+ IT Camp (Ministry of Youth & Sports × Turkcell Academy).
- Open-Meteo API: city search, hourly & 7-day forecasts with Loading / Success / Empty / Error states
- Weather-reactive dynamic theme, **Canvas-animated mascot**, favorites, location lookup, home-screen widget — **56 unit tests**

### 🌍 ÖlçKazan — AI Food Waste Measurement for Cafeterias
**COP31 Climate Tech Creathon · Top 10**

Measures food waste in institutional cafeterias with AI — no extra hardware — and helps plan the next day's production. Presented on the COP31 Türkiye (Antalya) stage.

### 🧒 TÜBİTAK 2209-A — Educational Mobile App
`Flutter`

Teaches kids how food is produced through quizzes, an AI chatbot, read-aloud and drag-and-drop. Published as a paper at **ICENSS 2026**.
